What I actually work with

Field instrumentation — my toolbox, and honestly, my happy place

Side hustle: getting hands-on with instruments in the lab alongside colleagues — genuinely one of the fun parts of this job.

SMPS

Sizes up aerosol particles by diameter — the bread and butter of aerosol counting.

SP2

Single-particle soot photometer — finds black carbon particle by particle.

CCNC

Cloud condensation nuclei counter — my main instrument, tells us which particles actually become droplets.

HTDMA

Watches particles swell as humidity rises — hygroscopic growth, up close.

POPS

Compact optical particle counter — small enough to take almost anywhere in the field.

OC/EC Analyser

Splits carbon in aerosol samples into organic vs. elemental — the "what's it made of" instrument.
